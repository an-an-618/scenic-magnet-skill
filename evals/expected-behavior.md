# Expected behavior

## Input contract

- One or more user-supplied reference photos.
- Optional exact text supplied by the user.
- No mandatory follow-up questions.

## Output contract

- One finished 4:3 landscape product image per requested source photo.
- One central metal micro-landscape fridge magnet, staged alone on a quiet tactile backing.
- Two to five source-grounded scene elements arranged as a coherent miniature landscape.
- A source-derived open contour rather than a default circle, shield, coin, or regular badge.
- A restrained metal system with no more than two metal colors, visible thickness, bevels, highlights, and contact shadow.
- At most one mechanism by default, and only when the source scene has a natural motion or reveal metaphor.
- No text unless it is exact user-provided text or clearly legible identity-bearing source text.
- No candidates, confirmation gates, hands, props, split before/after layout, watermark, or invented place identity.

## Quality threshold

The finished image must remain legible at thumbnail size, read as a manufactured object rather than a flat illustration, and retain enough spatial relationships that the source scene is recognizable without reproducing every detail.
