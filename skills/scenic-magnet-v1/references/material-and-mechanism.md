# Material and mechanism design

## Background material

Identify the source's largest effective color after ignoring clipped white sky, deep empty shadow, and small accents. Use that hue family for exactly one quiet material:

| Scene signal | Backing material |
| --- | --- |
| Coastal blue/cyan | silk paper or fine woven board |
| Botanical green | wool felt or fibrous paper |
| Warm ochre/orange architecture | corrugated art paper |
| Deep red transit or signage | woven paper or short-pile velvet |
| Neutral stone/urban gray | linen paper or museum board |

Use restrained lighting and low-contrast texture. The background supports metal highlights; it does not become a second subject. Do not add props, hands, refrigerators, stationery, packaging, or repeated decorative motifs.

## Metal system

Choose one family and no more than two finishes:

| Scene character | Metal family |
| --- | --- |
| Warm, historic, nostalgic | antique brass + restrained warm gold |
| Cool coast, modern city, airy daylight | brushed nickel + darkened silver |
| Mountain, industrial, night, rugged terrain | gunmetal + optional oxidized copper |

Create manufactured depth through cast edge thickness, stepped relief planes, shallow enamel recesses, bevel highlights, minor machining variation, and a soft directional contact shadow. Use fine colored enamel only where it supports the source's decisive color blocks. Avoid chrome overload, plastic gloss, toy-like inflation, and uniformly outlined clip art.

## Open contour

Derive the silhouette from the scene: roofline, headland, tree canopy, tram profile, road curve, mountain ridge, or facade corner. A small structural base may connect fragile parts. Do not default to a circle, shield, crest, coin, or rectangular picture frame.

## Mechanism gate

Add a mechanism only when all four checks pass:

1. The source visibly implies motion, change, passage, or reveal.
2. A real axle, hinge, rail, slot, or pull tab can be shown.
3. The moving part reinforces an identity-bearing element.
4. The mechanism remains legible without disassembling the product view.

If any check fails, use fixed relief. Default maximum: one mechanism. Use two only for a genuinely complex source when the mechanisms do not compete.

| Source semantics | Plausible mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Tram/train/vehicle on a route | short guided slider along track |
| Ferris wheel, windmill, compass, rotating sign | single pivot wheel |
| Door, window, book, reveal sign | miniature hinge |
| Tide, wave, rising sun, cable car | short sliding layer or pull tab |

Show the hardware. A moving part without a visible physical constraint reads as an illustration error.

## Text gate

Default to no text. Preserve exact user-provided wording. Preserve source lettering only when it is clearly legible, identity-bearing, and short enough to cast cleanly. Never infer missing letters, translate, correct spelling, or fabricate a caption.
